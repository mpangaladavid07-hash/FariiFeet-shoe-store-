# UI/UX Design Inspiration & Extraction Brief — Farii Feet

Keep screenshots of the two Dribbble reference designs inside this `media/ui-ux-inspiration/` folder so Cline (`@ui-ux-pro-max` & `@frontend-design`) can inspect them visually alongside your shoe photos.

---

## 1. Hero Section & Storefront Inspiration
- **Reference:** *Shoes Website Landing Page Design ✦ Heepzy* by Sahil Dobariya (Dribbble Shot #27595026)
- **URL:** https://dribbble.com/shots/27595026-Shoes-Website-Landing-Page-Design-Heepzy
- **What to Extract for Farii Feet:**
  - High-impact, editorial sneaker **Hero Section** that breaks away from generic e-commerce templates.
  - Bold oversized display typography layered behind/beside a featured hero sneaker cutout or lifestyle shot.
  - High-contrast pairing of **Obsidian Black (`#0D0C0B`)** and **Espresso Brown (`#453A33`)** surfaces with floating glassmorphism badges and quick-drop call-to-action buttons.

---

## 2. Website Layout & Product Page Structure
- **Reference:** *Zero-Waste Website: Product Page* by Tubik Studio (Dribbble Shot #7801805)
- **URL:** https://dribbble.com/shots/7801805-Zero-Waste-Website-Product-Page
- **What to Extract for Farii Feet:**
  - Clean split-screen **Product Detail & Size Matrix Layout**: large visual gallery on the left, structured product title, TZS pricing, EU size selector (36–45), and WhatsApp/Cart CTA on the right.
  - Balanced alternating light/dark editorial rhythm using **Blush Dusty Pink (`#E2BBC7`)** and **Soft Rose (`#F6F2F3`)** cards against deep black and warm brown tones.
  - Clear visual hierarchy, generous whitespace, and tactile product storytelling cards.

## How to Add Your Reference Images Here
1. Open the two Dribbble links above in your browser.
2. Take full-page or section screenshots (`Win + Shift + S` on Windows or `Cmd + Shift + 4` on Mac).
3. Save them inside this exact folder (`media/ui-ux-inspiration/`) as:
   - `hero-heepzy-reference.png`
   - `layout-tubik-reference.png`
